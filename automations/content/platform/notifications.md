# Notifications

A live flow runs without you watching it, so **FlowRunner™** tells you when something happened that you
would want to know about - a run that terminated, a payment that failed, a limit you are close to. Those
messages collect in the notification panel, and this page is about reading them and keeping the list under
control.

## Opening the panel

The bell in the top-right of the console opens the panel down the right-hand side. The number beside the
bell is how many messages are waiting, so you can tell at a glance whether anything arrived without opening
anything.

![The notification panel: a header reading "Notifications" with a count of 23 beside it and an "Unread only" link under it, two icon buttons at the top right, then a group headed "DF DOCUMENTATION FLOWS" holding a column of entries. Each entry has a dot, the bold title Flow "TD Sandbox" Terminated, a relative time such as "16 minutes ago", the line "A flow instance was terminated.", and a small grey chip reading flow-execution.](../images/platform/notifications-panel.png)

Clicking anywhere outside the panel closes it again. The second icon in its header pins it open instead, so
it survives moving between screens - useful while you are watching a flow you have just started, and the
reason the panel is sometimes already open when you arrive.

## What a notification tells you

Every entry carries the same four things, and each earns its place:

- **The title** - what happened, naming the flow it happened to, so a glance down the column is enough to
  spot the one that matters.
- **When it arrived**, written as time elapsed ("about 1 hour ago") rather than a timestamp.
- **A line of detail** under the title.
- **A source chip** - the part of the platform the message came from, such as `flow-execution`. This is
  what separates "a run of mine ended badly" from "something about the account needs attention", and it is
  the fastest way to skim a long list for one kind of message.

A dot at the start of a row marks it as one you have not read yet.

## Notifications from more than one workspace

The panel is per person, not per workspace, so it carries messages from every workspace you belong to.
They are grouped under a workspace heading, and the groups are what you scan first: a message under a
workspace you are not working in today is still yours to see, and still in its own group rather than mixed
into the flow you are looking at.

## Keeping the list short

Two controls sit in the panel header:

- The link under the title switches which messages are listed. It reads ((Unread only)) while you are
  seeing everything, and ((All notifications)) once you have narrowed it - each time, the label names the
  view you would move to.
- ((Mark read)) at the end of a workspace's group clears that workspace's unread messages out of the list
  in one go, rather than making you work down them one at a time.
