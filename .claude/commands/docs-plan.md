You are planning the concept page for: **$ARGUMENTS**. Do NOT start writing the page yet — front-load the educator thinking that keeps getting skipped.

Read `automations/docs-review/CONCEPT-PAGE-PATTERN.md` (sections 0–0d) and `automations/block-knowledge/VOICE.md` first. Then:

1. **Interview Mark** (2–4 questions, via AskUserQuestion): the concept's ROLE in one line; what the reader must already know and from which earlier Learn-nav page; the ONE idea they must leave with; anything commonly misunderstood. Do not draft the frame cold.

2. **Map prior knowledge.** List what earlier pages in the Learn nav already taught that this page builds on (never re-explain or forward-reference).

3. **Write the lesson plan** to `/Users/mark/.claude/plans/` (or the page's ledger scratch): the idea in one breath; what the reader can DO after; a **value/outcome-first lede** (never mechanics); and a section outline where each section names its ONE takeaway (a meaningful-anchor title) and the real scenario its screenshot will show. For every product claim the plan will make, note that it must be verified in-product.

4. **Review the plan** before writing: dispatch one Agent (subagent) as the **educator lens** — give it the plan text + `CONCEPT-PAGE-PATTERN.md` and ask it to find where the plan still opens on mechanics, has weak/anchor-less titles, combines two ideas in a section, or assumes unproven product facts. Apply its findings.

5. Present the reviewed plan to Mark for approval. Only after approval do you write the page — then take it through `/docs-review` until `ship`.
